00

Ground rules

Seven rules. Each one is checkable, and tools/check.mjs checks the ones a machine can.

  1. Two line weights.

    2px frames what you press or what contains. 1px only divides. A heavier 4px exists for forced-colours and nothing else.

  2. Flat.

    No gradients, no blur, no glass. A shadow is hard-edged and means “you can press this”.

  3. Rectangles hold, circles act.

    Containers are rectangles, square or soft. Buttons and handles are circles or pills.

  4. Uppercase is structure.

    Bar labels, index numbers and the display line. Everything you read is sentence case.

  5. Say something real.

    No lorem ipsum, no “John Doe”, no “seamless”. Copy comes from the app.

  6. Colour is a swap.

    Components read roles: --paper, --ink, --tone-3. Only tokens/color.*.css knows a hex.

  7. No decoration.

    If it carries no information and offers no action, it goes. No dotted backgrounds, no icons in tinted circles.

  1. 1
    Frame.card

    2px, --card-line. Clips its contents, and is the container that cqi and @container card measure against.

  2. 2
    Bar.card__bar

    The frame made thicker: ink fill, a label on the left, one control on the right. The control is a 44px target around a 20px box.

  3. 3
    Media.card__media

    Edge to edge, 4:3 unless --ratio says otherwise. Closed off by a 2px rule, never a shadow.

  4. 4
    Body.card__body

    Where the padding lives: clamp(1rem, 5cqi, 1.5rem), so a narrow card is snug and a wide one breathes.

  5. 5
    Eyebrow.card__eyebrow

    14px, soft ink. Says what kind of thing this is.

  6. 6
    Title.card__title

    Weight 500, 22 to 32px depending on the card's width. Balanced, so it never leaves one word on its own line.

  7. 7
    Text.card__text

    16px on a 1.5 line, 40 characters wide at most.

  8. 8
    Foot.card__foot

    A 1px rule, then the small print. Sticks to the bottom, so cards in a row end on one line.

  9. 9
    Action.card__action

    A 44px circle. Round means it does something. On a link card it's decoration; in a notch it's a real button.

06

Not this

What the sheet is steering away from. The left card is deliberately generic, and it's the only place on this page where the rules are broken on purpose.

  1. Blur shadows, two of them, soft. Ours are hard or absent.
  2. Gradient fills. Ours are flat.
  3. An icon in a tinted circle that says nothing. Ours: circles are for actions.
  4. A coloured stripe down the left edge. Ours: the frame is the frame.
  5. One mid-size radius on everything. Ours: containers are square or soft, controls are circles or pills.
  6. Copy that fits any product. Ours comes from the app.
